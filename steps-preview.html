Premium Features

✨ Specular Highlights

Curved glass lens effect at the top of each card, simulating light refraction on actual glass surfaces.

🎨 Liquid Gradient Border

Dynamic gradient border that adapts the light and dark edges, creating depth perception.

📐 Backdrop Filter Blur

Strong 24px blur with 180% saturation ensuring content behind shows through realistically.

🎯 Interactive States

Pending (frosted), Active (luminous), Completed (subtle) states with smooth spring animations.

🎪 Double Shadow

Outer soft shadow for depth + inner rim light for 3D curvature perception.

♿ Accessible
